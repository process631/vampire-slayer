HOW TO SURVIVE

Move with WASD or arrow keys. On a phone, touch and drag anywhere. Your weapons fire by themselves.
Collect soul gems dropped by the dead to level up. Each level, pick 1 of 3 blessings: a new weapon, a weapon upgrade, or a passive relic. Keys 123 pick, R rerolls.
Evolve weapons: raise a weapon to its max level while owning its paired relic, and a golden Evolution appears. Elites and bosses drop relic chests.
Pickups: ♥ hearts heal, magnets pull every gem, holy bombs smite the screen, gold buys upgrades in the Crypt. Smash braziers for loot.
The Oracle watches your hunt. Press Consult (E) for a timed omen. When leveling, type a wish into the forge to craft your own blessing instead of taking a card.
Bosses rise at 3:00, 6:00 and 9:00. Survive until 10:00 and the dawn wins the night. Then the endless night awaits, if you dare.
